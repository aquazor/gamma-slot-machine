GAMMA LOOT ROULETTE — HOW IT WORKS
====================================
Inspired by Chaos Tricks. Mostly written by Claude. The app and this mode
are still a work in progress, but it already works quite well. Found a bug?
Message me on Discord — aquazor or Aquazor#1208 (not sure which one is correct)


SETUP
------
Two separate pieces, both needed:

1. GAMMA Randomizer Slot Machine by rip_perri — this is the in-game mod.
   Install it with MO2 like any other regular mod.
   -- IMPORTANT -- 
   * DO NOT CHANGE THE NAME OF THE MOD.
   * CHOSE Anomaly Launcher WHEN RUNNING GAMMA. Debug mode is not required. 

2. GAMMA Slot Machine App — this is the app that runs the roulette itself.
   Launch it and the Settings page opens automatically in your browser.
   Log in with your Twitch account there to activate the events (subs,
   gift subs, channel points, etc.) — without logging in, nothing on
   Twitch will trigger a roll. The app runs locally on your own machine,
   at localhost:7770.


WHAT IS THIS?
--------------
A Twitch-powered roulette that reacts live to what happens on stream:
channel point redemptions, subs, gift subs, and (optionally) bit cheers.
Depending on what triggered it, the roulette either:

  - rolls LOOT — a weapon, a helmet, and/or an armor set for the streamer, or
  - SPAWNS something in-game — a pack of mutants or a hostile squad near the
    streamer.

Every roll plays out live on stream as an animated slot-machine overlay with
spinning reels, sound, and sparkle effects before landing on a result.


HOW TO TRIGGER A ROLL
-----------------------
1. CHANNEL POINTS — three redeemable rewards, each costing channel points:
     "[SPIN] Spawn Squads"   — spawns a hostile squad
     "[SPIN] Spawn Mutants"  — spawns a mutant pack
     "[SPIN] Loot Roll"      — rolls gear for the streamer

2. BITS POWER-UPS — the same three rolls, but funded by bit cheers instead
   of channel points (the streamer sets the bit price on Twitch's side).
   These work the same way as the channel-point rewards above. Note: this
   is a limited-time Twitch event feature, and Twitch is retiring it on
   September 18, 2026 — after that date, this trigger goes away.

3. SUBS, RESUBS & GIFT SUBS — every subscription-related event rolls
   something automatically, no redemption needed:
     - A brand-new sub, a resub, or a single gifted sub has EQUAL odds
       (1-in-3 each) of rolling: loot, a squad, or a mutant pack — with a
       random 1-3 count either way (1-3 loot items, or 1-3 squad/mutant
       groups in Random mode).
     - Gifting MORE THAN ONE sub at once (a "gift bomb") still has those
       same 1-in-3 odds between loot/squad/mutant — it's not guaranteed to
       be a spawn. What changes is the SIZE and the ODDS once it lands:
         > It always uses the maximum count of 3 instead of a random 1-3
           (3 loot items, or 3 independently-rolled squad/mutant groups in
           Random mode).
         > If it lands on a squad or mutant spawn while Count Roll mode is
           active, that spawn is also GUARANTEED to land a bonus (see
           "Spawn Bonuses" below) — Count Roll always does a single roll
           regardless of gift size, so the "always 3" part above doesn't
           apply there; only the guaranteed bonus does.
       The size of the gift bomb itself (2 subs, 5 subs, 50 subs...)
       doesn't change any of this further — every gift bomb bigger than 1
       gets exactly the same treatment, however big it is.

4. BIT CHEERS — currently turned off. When enabled, cheering a set number of
   bits in chat triggers a roll the same way a channel point redemption
   would, based on how many bits were cheered.

5. MANUAL — the streamer can also fire any roll on demand from the Settings
   page, for testing or just for fun.


LOOT ROLLS
-----------
A loot roll spins 1 to 3 items for the streamer — any mix of a weapon, a
helmet, and body armor. The streamer picks a difficulty/quality preset
(Basic, Advanced, or Expert) in Settings, which controls how good the rolled
gear can be — Expert rolls noticeably better gear than Basic.


SPAWN ROLLS — TWO MODES
-------------------------
When a roll spawns mutants or a squad, it can work one of two ways. The
streamer switches between them any time in Settings, and it affects every
future spawn immediately.

RANDOM MODE
  The classic version. The roll independently picks 1 to 3 creature/squad
  groups (the same group can even come up more than once), each with its own
  spawn count. Simple and fast.

COUNT ROLL MODE  (currently the default and recommended)
  A more "slot machine" feeling version, with two separate spinning reels:
    - The first reel rolls a NUMBER — how many are about to spawn.
    - The second reel rolls WHAT spawns — which mutant type or which squad.
  Only one type of thing spawns per roll (never a mix), but the count can
  still be as small as 1 or fairly large, and — unlike Random mode — Count
  Roll spawns have a chance at a bonus (below).

  Note: because Count Roll always does a single "what + how many" roll, the
  channel-point/bits reward descriptions that mention rolling "1-3" packs
  are describing Random mode's behavior — while Count Roll is active, those
  same rewards still work, they just always produce one roll instead.


SPAWN BONUSES  (Count Roll mode only)
----------------------------------------
Every Count Roll spawn has a small chance of landing a bonus on top of the
normal result. At most one bonus can apply per roll. There are three kinds:

  x2   DOUBLE COUNT   — doubles however many were about to spawn
  +2   PLUS TWO       — adds 2 more to the spawn count
  RARE TIER UPGRADE   — instead of a normal pick, spawns something from the
                        NEXT tier up (e.g. a Basic roll can spawn something
                        that's normally only available at Advanced). Expert
                        rolls upgrade within Expert's own rarest options,
                        since there's no tier above Expert.

By default, each of the three bonuses has a 3.3% chance per roll (about a
1-in-10 chance of ANY bonus landing, and a 9-in-10 chance of a plain roll).
The streamer can:
  - turn any bonus on or off entirely, and
  - fine-tune each bonus's exact chance (as a percentage),
  from the Settings page — and those percentage tweaks are saved permanently
  (they survive the app being closed and reopened). If the streamer sets the
  enabled bonuses' chances to add up to 100% or more, a bonus becomes
  GUARANTEED on every roll (split fairly between whichever bonuses are
  enabled, based on their relative chances).

A landed bonus is impossible to miss on stream: the whole roulette glows and
pulses gold while it plays out, a "BONUS" badge appears, the number reel
shows the roll it landed on with the bonus called out (e.g. "x2 (x2
bonus)"), and the final result underneath shows the true total (e.g. "x4").

As mentioned above, any gift-sub bomb (more than 1 sub gifted at once) that
rolls a spawn ALWAYS lands one of the currently-enabled bonuses — guaranteed,
not just a higher chance.

Tier Upgrade specifically respects the same faction restrictions described
below — a Tier Upgrade can never pull in Monolith, Sin, or UNISG unless the
roll is already happening at the Expert tier.


DIFFICULTY TIERS: BASIC / ADVANCED / EXPERT
----------------------------------------------
Both mutant packs and hostile squads come in three tiers, switchable any
time in Settings, affecting every spawn until changed again. Higher tiers
generally mean stronger, tougher, and more varied enemies.

MUTANT PACKS BY TIER
  Basic:    Dogs, Pseudodogs, Boars, Cats, Fractures, Zombies, Snorks,
            Flesh, Lurkers, Poltergeist, Tushkano, Karlik
  Advanced: everything in Basic, PLUS: Bloodsuckers, Burers, Chimeras,
            Psysuckers, Psy Dog
  Expert:   everything in Advanced, PLUS: Strong Chimera, Gigant, Controller

HOSTILE SQUADS BY TIER
  Basic & Advanced: Renegades, Bandits, Loners, Mercs, Military, Duty,
            Freedom, Clear Sky, Ecolog, Zombied
  Expert:   everything above, PLUS: Monolith, Sin, UNISG

Monolith, Sin, and UNISG are exclusive to the Expert tier — they can never
show up while the squad difficulty is set to Basic or Advanced, even via a
Tier Upgrade bonus.


TURNING OFF SPECIFIC SQUADS OR MUTANTS
------------------------------------------
The streamer can individually disable any hostile squad faction (say, never
wanting Duty to spawn) from Settings — a disabled faction is skipped
entirely and can never come up, in either Random or Count Roll mode, and the
on/off state is shared between both modes (turning a squad off is a single
switch, not a separate one per mode).

There's currently no equivalent on/off switch for individual mutant types —
the mutant roster for each tier is fixed.


THE OVERLAY (WHAT VIEWERS SEE)
----------------------------------
Every roll appears as an animated overlay on stream: a badge at the top
shows who/what triggered it (Channel Points, Bits, New Sub, Resub, Gift Sub,
or Manual) and the viewer's name, then one or two spinning reels play out
with sound and sparkle effects before landing on the final result. If a
bonus lands, the whole thing gets a distinct gold glow and a "BONUS" badge
so it's obvious at a glance that something special just happened.


A NOTE ON ODDS
-----------------
"Chance" numbers above (3.3% per bonus, 1-in-3 category odds on subs, etc.)
reflect the CURRENT settings as of this guide. Bonus chances and which
squads are enabled can be changed by the streamer at any time from Settings,
so the exact odds in effect on stream may differ from the defaults listed
here if they've been tuned since.
